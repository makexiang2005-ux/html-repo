Performance experience

Kexiang Ma

Ma Kexiang is a Chinese singer. In December 2011, he participated in the recording of the program "My Creativity, My Choice." In September 2015, he played Wang Erxiao in CCTV-1's "The First Lesson of the New School Year" and performed "The Song of Wang Erxiao, the Cowherd" with Phoenix Legend. In 2016, he won the Golden Man Award for his performance of "Running" on the program "Music Express." In February 2017, he participated in the recording of the program "Super Simple" and performed "My Mother in My Dreams."
